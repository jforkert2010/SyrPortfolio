About Me

I recently completed my M.S. in Applied Data Science from Syracuse University, where I developed strong skills in data analysis, machine learning, business analytics, data warehousing, and visualization. Throughout the program, I worked with a wide range of tools and technologies including Python, SQL, R, Excel, Tableau, Spark, and database management systems to solve real-world business problems through data-driven solutions.

My coursework included classes such as IST 687 (Introduction to Data Science), IST 659 (Data Administration Concepts and Database Management), IST 707 (Applied Machine Learning), and IST 718 (Big Data Analytics). These courses allowed me to complete projects involving data collection, data cleaning, predictive modeling, database design, business intelligence, and decision-making strategies. Many of those projects are featured throughout this portfolio.

One of the most valuable parts of my graduate program was applying data science across diverse industries and problem spaces. From finance and healthcare to environmental research and supply chain analytics, I developed the ability to translate complex data into actionable insights. I enjoy identifying patterns, solving problems, and building solutions that improve decision-making and operational efficiency.

In addition to my academic experience, my professional background in healthcare and analytical chemistry has strengthened my attention to detail, statistical reasoning, and process improvement mindset. Working in regulated environments has taught me the importance of data accuracy, documentation, and collaboration across teams.

I am passionate about using data to solve meaningful problems and continuously expanding my technical and analytical skills. This portfolio highlights projects that reflect both my academic journey and my commitment to practical, results-driven data science.
