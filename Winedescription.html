Wine Quality Rating Prediction

Summary: Analyzed Portuguese Vinho Verde wine data, using chemical attributes (pH, sulfates, acidity) to predict quality scores (0–10) and identify key drivers of wine quality. I used R to clean and analyze the dataset, then applied visualizations like boxplots and correlation matrices to identify which chemical attributes most impacted wine quality. I also built a decision tree model to predict quality scores. The key outcome was identifying the most influential variables, which could help producers focus on optimizing those factors.sulfates, etc.

Goal/Objectives: Determine which available measurements would indicate high wine Quality

Course: IST 687
