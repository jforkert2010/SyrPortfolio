Financial Fraud Prediction

Our objective is to use machine learning techniques to increase efficacy in flagging fraudulent transactions based on behavior patterns for smaller credit companies. The metrics for improvement are increasing flags on actual fraud while not over-flagging legitimate transactions resulting in customer inconvenience. We believe this will reduce fraudulent transactions processed and the total cost incurred from fraud. Moreover, our ability to protect account identities will increase customer trust.
