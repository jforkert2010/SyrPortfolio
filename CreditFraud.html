Financial Fraud Prediction

This coursework placed a strong emphasis on machine learning, with IST 707 providing a deeper and more comprehensive understanding of core concepts, including entropy and model evaluation techniques. Throughout the course, I explored a wide range of machine learning methods, such as Random Forests, Decision Trees, K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), Support Vector Classifiers (SVC), Logistic Regression, Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD), and XGBoost.

For the final project, our team analyzed a simulated financial fraud dataset to identify patterns and key indicators of fraudulent activity. Given the significant financial and societal impact of fraud, this project aimed to generate actionable insights while maintaining ethical data practices.

Using Python, we performed data cleaning and preprocessing, followed by exploratory data analysis. We created visualizations to examine the distribution of transaction types and identify trends, such as which days of the week fraudulent transactions were more likely to occur. Additionally, we developed a correlation matrix to highlight the relationships between variables and their potential influence on fraud detection.

We implemented multiple machine learning models and evaluated their performance using metrics such as the F1 score, which proved especially valuable in balancing precision and recall for this classification problem. Our analysis revealed that the most significant predictors of financial fraud were transaction amount and type of transfer.

Ethical considerations were a key component of this project. Due to the sensitive nature of financial data, we used a simulated dataset to protect privacy. We also examined potential biases in fraud detection models, recognizing that automated systems can inadvertently introduce fairness and accuracy concerns.

To summarize our findings, we created a comparative table highlighting the performance of each model, allowing us to identify the most effective approaches for fraud detection.

Through this project, I strengthened my understanding of machine learning workflows, model evaluation, and the importance of ethical considerations in data-driven decision-making.
